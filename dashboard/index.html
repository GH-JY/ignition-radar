The model

One function, every protocol

implied = TVL × 0.30 × sector × growth × tenure

growth  = 1 + clamp(Δ7d, −50%, +150%) ÷ 100 × 0.55
tenure  = 0.55 + min(age, 2555 d) ÷ 2555 d × 0.65

The sector multiple is the share of TVL a launch in that sector tends to price in. Growth reads the last seven days; tenure rewards a protocol that has run longer without a token, because tenure is the record a launch is priced against. An unknown age uses one year. No protocol name is special-cased.

The signal chip is the board’s own split: the strongest fifth of a mix of momentum, tenure and size is marked strong, the next three tenths building, the rest early. It orders attention; it does not predict a launch.

The check

The page re-runs the arithmetic

The script beside this page recomputes every row from the snapshot figures it prints — TVL, change, age, sector — and compares the result with the number the board carries. Open any protocol: the detail view shows the three multipliers, the multiplication, and a check chip that turns green only when the page's number equals the board's.

Farm plans

Six steps, a cost and a time each

Each row's plan is written from its own record: the chain comes from the protocol's chain list, the position size from its TVL tier, the hold window from its age, and the actions from its sector. A lending market gets a supply-and-borrow plan; a bridge gets a bridge-and-return plan; a young protocol gets a longer hold. Every step carries a cost and a time, and the last step is always the safe one: claim only from the protocol's own site and keep a log.
